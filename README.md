# Galli Cricket

A pixel-art 3D street cricket game that runs in the browser, built with Three.js in a single HTML file.

**Play:** https://muhammadalims.github.io/galli-cricket/

## Grounds
- **The Gully**: a narrow lane between houses. Ball in a house or through a window is out.
- **The Chhat**: a rooftop. No running; parapet is 1 or 2, a water tank is 4, off the roof is out.
- **Juhu Beach**: soft sand. In the sea on the full is out, over the promenade is six.
- **Society Parking**: stilt parking. Touch the ceiling or hit a car on the full and you're out.

## Modes
Bat against Chintu, bowl at Chintu, or 2 players on one keyboard or phone.

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** Space to swing and run, arrows to aim, hold up to loft. Bowling: arrows (WASD in 2 players) to aim, Q/E for the ball, Space (F) to bowl and lock.
- **Phone (landscape):** left stick to aim, right button to swing, run, bowl and lock.

## Development
Edit `game.html`, then run `./build.sh` to regenerate `index.html`.
